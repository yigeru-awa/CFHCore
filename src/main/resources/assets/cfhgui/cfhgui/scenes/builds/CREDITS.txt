The builds in this folder are other people's work, shipped with CFHGui on their authors' terms (see docs/scene-credits.md
in Slate's repository for the full story). Each file is the part of the build the main menu's camera shows, cut from the
author's download; blocks no one can see are filled with stone, and in Red rose city the builders' scaffolds floating in
the sky are left out; nothing else is changed.

red_rose_city.schem    Red rose city, by Dominic_de_Vos (vintageblocks) and the Red rose city team.
                       https://www.planetminecraft.com/project/lma-cathedral/
                       "You can use the map for any purposes, modify it, or use parts of it as basis for whatever you want
                       to create."

rungholt.schem         Neu Rungholt, by kescha.
                       https://www.planetminecraft.com/project/neu-rungholt/
                       "Feel free to use the map or parts of it for you own projects."

sky_fortress.litematic Sky Island Elven Fortress, by Mafia, Cloudax and Laoevpes.
                       https://www.planetminecraft.com/project/beautiful-sky-island-mega-base-elvin-fortress/
                       "If you decide to use the build, just make sure to credit the above. Other then that, feel free to
                       use it as your own and to make changes, as long as you do not re-upload it on this site."
